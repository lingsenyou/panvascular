Results

Where the data comes from. Records are queried live from the OpenAlex API, an open index of scholarly works released under CC0. Only DOI-bearing records are shown. Crossref is then used to verify the DOI and, where available, correct the title, byline, journal and publication year. This page stores no corpus of its own and produces no machine-written summaries. A DOI missing from Crossref may belong to another registration agency, so the page reports that status instead of silently treating it as verified.

On citation counts. Indexes genuinely disagree, so each figure carries the name of the index that produced it. Where a DOI is present the Crossref cited-by tally is fetched as well. Either index may be higher because their coverage and update schedules differ. Google Scholar is never collected automatically; the Scholar link simply hands the title to its public search so readers can inspect that source themselves.

Built and maintained by Lingsen You, Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University. Query endpoint: api.openalex.org/works. This static public version uses OpenAlex's no-key allowance, so temporary rate limiting is possible during heavy use.
