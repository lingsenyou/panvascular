How this page works

Search runs against the PubMed E-utilities, which are free, keyless and have no daily budget, so the radar keeps working however many people use it. Citation counts, open-access status, field-weighted citation impact and retraction flags come from OpenAlex, one free single-record lookup per paper. When OpenAlex is unreachable the cards simply show no citation figure rather than a guess.

The first screen is a snapshot baked into this page, so it reads instantly and still works offline or when an API is down. Its date is always stated. Nothing on this page is written or ranked by a language model, and no summary is generated: every abstract is the author's own, as deposited in PubMed.
